- $\nabla \Delta L_{lk,f}^{i,j}(k) = \rho_{lk}^{i,j}(k) + \lambda_{f} N_{lk,f}^{i,j} + d \rho_{lk}^{i,j}(k) - I_{lk,f}^{i,j}(k) + T_{lk}^{i,j}(k) + \varepsilon_{lk,f}^{i,j}(k)$
- $\nabla \Delta L_{lk,f}^{i,j}(k-1) = \rho_{lk}^{i,j}(k-1) + \lambda_{f} N_{lk,f}^{i,j} + d \rho_{lk}^{i,j}(k-1) - I_{lk,f}^{i,j}(k-1) + T_{lk}^{i,j}(k-1) + \varepsilon_{lk,f}^{i,j}(k-1)$
- 二者再次求差得：
	- ## $\Delta L_{lk,f}^{i,j}(k,k-1) = \Delta \rho_{lk}^{i,j}(k,k-1) + \Delta \varepsilon_{lk,f}^{i,j}(k,k-1)$
