# $\varphi_{\text{wide-lane}} = \varphi_{L1} - \varphi_{L2}$

^9c884a

- $f_{\text{wide-lane}} = f_{L1} - f_{L2} = 347.82 \text{MHz}$
- $\lambda_{\text{wide-lane}} = \frac{c}{f_{\text{wide-lane}}} = 86.19 \text{cm}$
- $N_{\text{wide-lane}} = N_{L1} - N_{L2}$
- $\sigma_{\rho_{\text{wide-lane}}} = 86.19 \text{cm} \cdot \sqrt{2} \cdot \sigma_{\varphi} = 1.22 \text{cm}$

### 特点
- 波长较长，[[整周模糊度|模糊度]]具有整数特性，利于[[整周模糊度|模糊度解算]]，但测量噪声被放大
