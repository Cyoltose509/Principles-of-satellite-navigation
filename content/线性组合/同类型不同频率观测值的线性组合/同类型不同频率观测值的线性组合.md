- 一般形式
- $\varphi_{n,m} = n \cdot \varphi_{L1} + m \cdot \varphi_{L2}$
	- 频率特性：$f_{n,m} = n \cdot f_{L1} + m \cdot f_{L2}$
	- 波长特性：$\lambda_{n,m} = \frac{c}{f_{n,m}} = \frac{c}{n \cdot f_{L1} + m \cdot f_{L2}} = \frac{1}{\frac{n}{\lambda_{L1}} + \frac{m}{\lambda_{L2}}} = \frac{\lambda_{L1} \lambda_{L2}}{n \lambda_{L2} + m \lambda_{L1}}$
	- [[整周模糊度]]特性：$N_{n,m} = n \cdot N_{L1} + m \cdot N_{L2}$
	- [[电离层误差|电离层延迟]]特性：$(\delta \varphi_{ion})_{n,m} = n \cdot (\delta \varphi_{ion})_{L1} + m \cdot (\delta \varphi_{ion})_{L2} = n \cdot (-\frac{A}{cf_{L1}}) + m \cdot (-\frac{A}{cf_{L2}}) = -A \cdot \frac{n \cdot f_{L2} + m \cdot f_{L1}}{cf_{L1} \cdot f_{L2}}$
	- [[对流层误差|对流层延迟]]特性：
		- $(\delta \varphi_{\text{trop}})_{n, m} = \left( \frac{n}{\lambda_{L1}} + \frac{m}{\lambda_{L2}} \right) \cdot \delta \rho_{\text{trop}}$
		- $(\delta\rho_{\text{trop}})_{n,m} = \lambda_{n,m} \cdot (\delta\varphi_{\text{trop}})_{n,m} = \delta\rho_{\text{trop}}$
	- 误差特性：
		- $\sigma_{\varphi_{n,m}} = \left[\left(n\sigma_{\varphi_1}\right)^2 + \left(m\sigma_{\varphi_2}\right)^2\right]^{1/2}$
		- $\sigma_{\rho_{n,m}} = \lambda_{n,m} \cdot \sigma_{\varphi_{n,m}}$
